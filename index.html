About Us

Sunshine Vacations & Tourism is dedicated to offering the best travel experiences by helping you explore exotic destinations around the world. Our team of travel experts ensures that every trip is a memorable one, catering to your every need and creating unforgettable memories.

Our Packages

Kerala

Kerala, in South India, is famous for its backwaters, palm-lined beaches, and rich cultural traditions.

Tamil Nadu

Tamil Nadu, in southern India, is known for its Dravidian-style temples, beaches, and classical arts.

Karnataka

Karnataka is home to bustling cities, lush forests, and beautiful beaches, with a rich cultural history.

Kashmir

Kashmir, known as "Paradise on Earth," offers stunning landscapes, including valleys, lakes, and mountains.

Andaman

The Andaman Islands are known for their palm-lined, white-sand beaches, mangroves, and tropical rainforests.

Bali

Bali, Indonesia, is known for its forested volcanic mountains, iconic rice paddies, beaches, and coral reefs.

Baku

Baku, the capital of Azerbaijan, is famous for its medieval walled old city and contemporary architecture.

Bhutan

Bhutan, a Buddhist kingdom on the Himalayas' eastern edge, is known for its monasteries, fortresses, and dramatic landscapes.

Dubai

Dubai, a city in the United Arab Emirates, is known for its modern architecture, luxury shopping, and vibrant nightlife.

Goa

Goa, a state in western India, is renowned for its beaches, Portuguese heritage, and vibrant nightlife.

Himachal

Himachal Pradesh is known for its scenic mountain towns, resorts, and trekking trails.

Nepal

Nepal, home to Mount Everest, offers scenic mountain views, trekking routes, and rich cultural heritage.

Leh Ladakh

Leh Ladakh, a region in northern India, is known for its stark landscapes and Buddhist monasteries.

Singapore

Singapore, a global financial hub, is known for its modern skyline, diverse culture, and lush green spaces.

Srilanka

Srilanka, an island nation, is famous for its ancient ruins, pristine beaches, and diverse wildlife.

North East

Meghalaya, in northeast India, is known for its living root bridges, caves, and high rainfall.

Maharashtra

Maharashtra is home to bustling cities, ancient cave temples, and beautiful hill stations.

Rajasthan

Rajasthan, a state in northern India, is famous for its deserts, palaces, and vibrant culture.

Sikkim + Darjeeling

Sikkim and Darjeeling are known for their tea gardens, scenic beauty, and vibrant culture.

Thailand

Thailand is known for its tropical beaches, opulent royal palaces, ancient ruins, and ornate temples.

Uttarakhand

Uttarakhand, known as the 'Land of the Gods,' is famous for its Hindu temples, trekking routes, and natural beauty.

Vietnam + Cambodia

Vietnam and Cambodia offer a blend of vibrant cities, ancient temples, and beautiful landscapes.
